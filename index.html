Why this is ready now

Disconnected requirements are already expensive

Requirements are written once, then copied by hand into four other systems. Every copy that drifts costs review cycles, team time and schedule.

Why nowThree shifts make a connected record practical, and more valuable, this year.

Regulatory scrutiny is rising

FDA scrutiny is growing, with Elsa adding AI-assisted review. Much of what review flags starts in development, not in the submission.

Requirements are still re-keyed by hand

Planning, QA and trackers each hold their own copy. When one moves and the others do not, the gap stays hidden until late.

The enabling tools are now mature

Platforms and AI can draft from the MRD, connect to the systems teams already use, and flag gaps while the work is happening.
